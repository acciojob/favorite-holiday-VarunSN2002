Canary Islands


The Canary Islands are a Spanish autonomous archipelago located in the Atlantic Ocean off the northwest coast of Africa. Known for their subtropical climate and year-round warm weather, they combine European infrastructure with diverse volcanic landscapes.

Top 5 attractions

  1. Mount Teide National Park
  2. Loro Parque
  3. Siam Park
  4. Maspalomas Dunes
  5. Timanfaya National Park

Must-try foods

  • Papas Arrugadas con Mojo
  • Gofio
  • Fresh Seafood
